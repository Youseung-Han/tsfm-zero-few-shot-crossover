# TSFM Crossover Results

CPU-only explorer of saved final-test NMAE for TTM-R3 and MOIRAI 1.1-R-small.
No inference, model weights, tokens or example/synthetic research results.
The curves show mean paired-seed improvement with seed min-max shading, not confidence intervals.

Source and reproduction: https://github.com/Han-Youseung/tsfm-zero-few-shot-crossover
